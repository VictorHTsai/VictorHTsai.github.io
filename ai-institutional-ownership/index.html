13F reports are filed quarterly, usually up to 45 days after quarter end. Checking every day can discover new filings, but cannot turn quarterly reported positions into daily confirmed holdings. 13F primarily reports long positions, not a hedge fund's complete net exposure. Price moves must not be misread as institutional fund flows.
